Please consider “barbac: fast DNA barcode clustering from reads to lineage
trajectories” for publication as an Original Paper in *Bioinformatics*.

Barbac combines a native abundance-aware barcode clustering engine with
indel-preserving extraction, R1 and overlapping paired-read processing,
time-series lineage visualization and a local Shiny interface. Across two
replicated simulated designs and a deposited benchmark, its LV configuration
has the highest exact-centroid F1 and read-assignment accuracy among the tested
configurations, while its Hamming configuration is fastest. The manuscript
reports paired comparisons, the scope of a completed Shepherd sensitivity
analysis, and a measured real-data workflow from FASTQ to saved plots.

The implementation, test examples, source code and reproducibility records are
available at https://github.com/loukesio/barbac. The submitted software/test-data
archive identifier will be inserted before submission. Experimental barcode
time series demonstrate the practical workflow beyond simulated benchmarks.

AI assistance (OpenAI Codex) was used during software development, benchmark
orchestration, documentation and manuscript preparation. The authors are
responsible for reviewing the analyses and final manuscript.
